Privacy Policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

Adams Lux Auto ("we", "us") rents luxury vehicles in the San Francisco Bay Area. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit adamsluxauto.com, request a quote (including through Facebook or Instagram lead forms), or book a car, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Contact details: your name, phone number and email address.
  • Trip details: the car you're interested in, rental dates, pickup or delivery location, and any notes you send us.
  • Booking and verification documents: when you book, your driver's license and proof of insurance, so we can confirm you're eligible to drive the vehicle.
  • Payment information: payments are processed by Stripe. We don't see or store your full card number.
  • Website usage: our site uses the Meta Pixel to measure how our ads perform.

How we use it

  • To send you a quote, answer your questions and arrange your rental.
  • To verify your identity and eligibility, and to process payments and deposits.
  • To contact you about your booking by text, phone or email.
  • To measure and improve our advertising.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers we need to run the business: Stripe (payments), Supabase (secure data storage), Google (email) and Meta (advertising and lead forms). We may also share information when required by law, or with insurers or authorities when needed to resolve an incident involving a rented vehicle.

How long we keep it

We keep quote requests and booking records only as long as needed to serve you and meet legal, tax and insurance obligations. Verification documents are stored privately and deleted once they're no longer needed.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information, or to stop contacting you, at any time. California residents have these rights under the CCPA/CPRA.

Contact

Email adam@adamsluxauto.com with any privacy questions or requests.
